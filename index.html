        How the hybrid AI works

        • On-device — Gemini Nano 4: instant clause scanner + “explain this clause”. Private by design: names, addresses and amounts never leave your device. Single-turn text generation.
        • Hybrid — prefer on-device: plain-English clause rewrite. Local first, cloud fallback, so it always works.
        • Cloud — Gemini Flash: full-document summary, risk score, negotiation checklist and draft email. Long-context reasoning the on-device model cannot do.
        • Every AI answer carries a badge showing where it actually ran, read from the SDK’s response.inferenceSource (ON_DEVICE / IN_CLOUD).
        • On-device needs Chrome 139+ on desktop with the flag chrome://flags/#prompt-api-for-gemini-nano-multimodal-input enabled and the model downloaded. Without it, the app falls back to the cloud.

        ClauseCheck explains — it does not give legal advice. For binding decisions, talk to a qualified lawyer. Built for the Firebase #BuildHybridAI 2026 hackathon with Firebase AI Logic (Gemini Nano on-device + Gemini Flash in the cloud).
